Do predicates and accounts constrain each other?

Not in the schema, but in practice some pairs fit much better than others.

Formally: independent

CAMO 0.8.0 has no rule linking predicate to philosophical_accounts. The only predicate rules involve causal_language. Any of the 88 combinations validates.

Conceptually: loosely coupled

The predicate says what is claimed; the account says what kind of causation that claim relies on. For the eight causal predicates most accounts are workable, and a few pairings are especially natural (mediates with mechanistic, moderates with INUS, prevents with counterfactual).

The real constraint: non-causal predicates

associated_with, correlated_with and precedes make no causal claim, yet philosophical_accounts is required on every edge. Only probabilistic (co-variation) and regularity (constant conjunction) describe what such evidence can show. Production, intervention, counterfactual and agency accounts all presuppose causation.

● Natural: a usual reading of this predicate ○ Possible: coherent, less common × Strained: the account presupposes more than the predicate claims
